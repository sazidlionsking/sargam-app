Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Sargam Player is free, and there is no account to create.

Does it come with songs?

No. Sargam Player plays the music and videos that are already on your phone. It does not download or stream songs.

Why does Android show a "Play Protect" message?

Because the app is installed outside the Play Store and is new to Google. Tap "Scan app" or "Install anyway". The app only reads the media you give it access to.

How do I update?

The app tells you when a new version is ready. Tap "Update now", download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your playlists stay.

Which Android versions work?

Sargam Player is built for modern Android phones. If it does not install on yours, please let us know.
